08 THE GOOD QUESTIONS

Questions,
answered.

Good seafood starts with knowing a little more.

What is SuperFrozen technology?+

SuperFrozen technology freezes seafood at −76°F (−60°C) immediately after catch, halting degradation and helping lock in just-caught color, texture, and taste. Unlike conventional freezing, it helps prevent the ice crystal formation that can damage cell structure.

Are your seafood products additive-free?+

Oddisea chooses clean and natural seafood and does not use additives to preserve its SuperFrozen seafood. Contact the team for product-specific ingredient and handling information.

What does vessel-to-vendor traceability mean?+

It means building transparency and accountability through the seafood supply chain, from source toward the customer. For product-specific sourcing details, please contact Oddisea directly.

Are all products certified?+

Certifications depend on the product. Oddisea offers specific MSC- and ASC-certified selections; ask the team for current product documentation and availability.

Do you offer custom programs?+

Oddisea partners with fine dining restaurants, premium retail and culinary leaders. Contact our team to discuss the needs of your seafood program.
